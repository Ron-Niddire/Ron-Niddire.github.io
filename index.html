Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? On your device. Optional iCloud sync keeps a copy in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I add the widget? Long-press your Home Screen → tap + → search "GLP-1 Jab Tracker". Lock Screen widgets are under Customise Lock Screen.
  • I bought Premium before — do I need to subscribe? No. Tap "Restore Purchases" on the Premium screen and your lifetime unlock will come back.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the "Manage Subscription" link in the app.
