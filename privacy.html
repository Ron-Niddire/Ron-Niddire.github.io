Privacy Policy

GLP-1 Jab Tracker — Last updated: August 2026

Overview

GLP-1 Jab Tracker ("the App") is developed by Ronnie Niddrie. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data Collection & Storage

All data you enter into the App — including injection logs, weight entries, body measurements, protein and water logs, side effect records, medication preferences, and progress photos — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate any servers and your data is never transmitted to us or any third party.

iCloud Sync (optional)

If you turn on "Sync with iCloud" in Settings, the App stores a copy of your data in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it can be restored on a new device. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple, is only accessible to you, and is never visible to the developer. Progress photos and Apple Health data are not synced. You can turn sync off at any time; deleting the App's data from iCloud can be done in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show a small summary (your next dose date, dose, suggested injection site and weight change). This summary is stored in the App's private shared container on your device only.

Reports & Exports

You can create a PDF report or CSV export of your data. These files are generated on your device and are only shared with whoever you choose to share them with using the iOS share sheet.

Apple HealthKit

The App can optionally read and write weight data to Apple Health (HealthKit) if you grant permission. This integration is entirely optional and controlled by you. The App will:

  • Read body mass (weight) data from HealthKit to display in your weight tracking chart
  • Write weight entries you log in the App to HealthKit

HealthKit data is never used for advertising, shared with third parties, or transmitted off your device. In accordance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines, HealthKit data is not stored in iCloud or any external database.

Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications to remind you of upcoming injections. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external server or push notification service.

Camera & Photo Library

The App may request access to your camera and photo library for the progress photos feature. Photos are stored locally on your device in the App's private documents directory. They are not uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional Premium subscription (monthly or yearly, with a free trial) via Apple's In-App Purchase system. Earlier one-time Premium purchases remain valid. All purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit; we do not collect or store any payment information. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period and can be managed in your App Store account settings.

Analytics & Advertising

The App does not include any analytics SDKs, tracking tools, or advertising frameworks. We do not collect usage data, device identifiers, or any form of analytics.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs beyond Apple's native frameworks (SwiftData, CloudKit, HealthKit, StoreKit, WidgetKit and UserNotifications).

Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete all App data by using the "Reset All Data" option in the App's Settings, or by deleting the App from your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
